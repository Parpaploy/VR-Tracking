# Admin dashboard composition brief

## Mode

Operate

## Approved composition

The user approved `.impeccable/mocks/dashboard-a.png` and requested that university names and seals be removed. Keep VR Tracker as the only product identity. They later chose the indigo shown in their reference image as the site-wide primary color and requested square corners throughout. This comp is the north star for layout and hierarchy; its sample names, dates, counts, equipment models, and claims are not approved content.

Use indigo `#2e2f70` for primary navigation, links, focus and primary actions. Preserve status colors: yellow for pending approval, red for borrowed, green for ready, and gray for damaged. Use square corners on every surface and control.

## First viewport

The dashboard opens with the equipment inventory register and pending approval register visibly above the inventory rows. Keep the compact summary strip and the first few inventory rows in view on desktop. Place each pending request's time, borrower, device and action together. Real data only.

## Translation

Use the app's existing student and admin data, device statuses, loan history, search, filters, image review, approval and QR return flows. Omit purpose, usage windows, equipment models, QR thumbnails, university logos, notification bell, and any fields or claims that do not exist in the product. Do not invent due dates or institutional identity. On narrow screens, preserve the record fields and actions in labeled stacked rows.
